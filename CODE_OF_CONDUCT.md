# Code of Conduct

TopoAstero contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, distinguish evidence from
opinion, credit prior work, disclose conflicts, and make technical review safe for disagreement.
Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, plagiarism, fabrication, and selective suppression of
unfavorable scientific results are not acceptable.

Concerns should be reported privately to the project lead or, when that person is involved, to the
supervisor or the relevant institutional research-integrity channel. Reports will be handled with
confidentiality consistent with safety and institutional obligations.
