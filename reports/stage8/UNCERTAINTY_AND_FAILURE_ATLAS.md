# Uncertainty and failure-domain atlas

Eight independent realization refits per evaluation cell cycle through three fitted-background branches. The 90% percentile interval uses a frozen minimum half-width of 12% of the point estimate; detectable-cell empirical coverage is 0.200. This is a finite-simulation coverage diagnostic, not a claim of universal calibration.

Six prespecified injected failure modes (glitch, curvature, dropout, avoided crossing, rotation, and noise) are recorded in `results/stage8/failure_atlas.csv`. The single representative stress case is diagnostic, not a population failure rate. Angular intervals are shortest empirical arcs on S1 and can wrap the displayed seam.
