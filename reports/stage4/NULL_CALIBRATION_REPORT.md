# Stage 4 null and multiplicity calibration

Seven nuisance-relevant null families were calibrated with 256 realizations and evaluated on 256 disjoint seeds per family. The decision threshold is the family-specific maximum across every registered trial spacing, smoothing scale, and summary. The largest independent empirical family-wise false-positive rate was **0.066** (gate: 0.080). Pointwise thresholds are explicitly diagnostic-only.

The calibration covers background-only, randomized-comb, white-noise, red-noise, phase-randomized, IAAFT, and payload-derived real-window nulls.
