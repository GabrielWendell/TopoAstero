# Stage 4 uncertainty and compute report

Split calibration/evaluation experiments cover 27 combinations of S/N, duration, and lifetime. Realization scatter and pipeline-choice scatter are recorded separately. Empirical coverage spans **0.912-0.972** for nominal 95% intervals.

Measured kernels cover 512, 2,048, and 8,192 samples for persistence, delay embedding, FFT spectra, and null-score ensembles. The largest median kernel time on this recorded environment was **0.0545 s**. Confirmation uses 64-realization chunks and repetition counts derived from binomial Monte Carlo standard-error targets.
